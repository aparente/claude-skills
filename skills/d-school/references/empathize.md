# Empathize Phase Reference

Tools for understanding users deeply before defining the problem.

---

## Interview Facilitation

### 5 Whys Technique
Ask "why" 5 times to move from surface symptoms to root causes.

**Facilitation:**
1. "Tell me about a recent time you experienced [challenge]."
2. After each answer, ask "Why is that?" or "Why does that matter to you?"
3. Track the chain: Surface → Layer 2 → Layer 3 → Layer 4 → Root

### Specific-Experience Probing
Never ask about generalities. Always ask about a specific instance.

**Prompts to Use:**
- "Tell me about the LAST time you [did the thing]."
- "Walk me through exactly what happened, step by step."
- "What were you thinking at that moment?"
- "What did you do right before? Right after?"
- "What was the hardest part? What was surprisingly easy?"

### Interview Do's and Don'ts
- DO: Ask open-ended questions ("Tell me about...")
- DO: Follow up on emotions ("You mentioned frustration — say more")
- DO: Ask for stories, not opinions
- DON'T: Ask leading questions ("Don't you think...?")
- DON'T: Ask yes/no questions
- DON'T: Suggest solutions during interviews

## Observation Framework: Says / Does / Thinks / Feels

Use when the user shares observations about their users.

| Quadrant | What to Capture | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **Says** | Direct quotes, stated needs | "What exact words did they use?" |
| **Does** | Observable behaviors, actions | "What did you see them actually do?" |
| **Thinks** | Inferred beliefs, mental models | "What do you think they believe about this?" |
| **Feels** | Emotions, motivations, fears | "What emotions did you observe or infer?" |

**Facilitation:**
1. "Let's map what you know about your users across 4 quadrants."
2. Fill each quadrant with 3-5 items
3. "Where do Says and Does conflict? That's where the deepest insights live."

## Empathy Map

Structured synthesis of user understanding.

```
EMPATHY MAP: [User/Persona Name]
────────────────────────────────
THINKS & FEELS
- [internal thoughts, worries, aspirations]

SEES
- [environment, market, what friends do]

SAYS & DOES
- [observable behavior, public statements]

HEARS
- [what friends, boss, influencers say]

────────────────────────────────
PAINS: [frustrations, obstacles, fears]
GAINS: [wants, needs, measures of success]
```

**Facilitation:**
1. "Who is the specific user we're mapping?"
2. Work through each section: "What does [user] think and feel about this space?"
3. "Now the crucial parts — what are their top 3 pains and gains?"

## User Journey Map

Track the user experience stage by stage.

**Template:**
```
STAGE: [Name]
Action: [what user does]
Touchpoint: [what they interact with]
Thought: [what they're thinking]
Emotion: [feel-good / neutral / frustrated]
Pain Point: [frustration at this stage]
Opportunity: [how this stage could improve]
```

**Facilitation:**
1. "What's the first thing the user does when they encounter this situation?"
2. Map 5-8 stages from trigger to completion
3. "Where are the emotional low points? Those are our design opportunities."

## Assumption Mapping

Before research, surface what you think you know vs. what you actually know.

**Matrix:**

```
                HIGH IMPORTANCE
                     |
    INVESTIGATE      |    VALIDATE
    (risky gaps)     |    (critical bets)
                     |
 LOW CERTAINTY ------+------ HIGH CERTAINTY
                     |
    PARK             |    LEVERAGE
    (low priority)   |    (known strengths)
                     |
                LOW IMPORTANCE
```

**Facilitation:**
1. "List 8-10 assumptions about your users, their needs, or your solution."
2. For each: "How important is this to your success? How certain are you?"
3. Place in the matrix
4. "Top-right (Validate) and top-left (Investigate) are your priorities."
